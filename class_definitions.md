#Class 0
Class name: Spalling
Positive definition: A visibly damaged concrete area where material is missing from the surrounding surface, creating an irregular, broken or recessed region.
Boundary cases: Scaling, honeycombing, chipping, cracks and exposed aggregate.
Box rule: One box around each visually distinct and continuous spalled area, including the complete visible area of material loss but minimizing surrounding undamaged concrete. Two boxes, if two damaged areas are clearly separated by intact concrete.
Minimum size: To define from the actual dataset resolution. In pixels, at a stated capture resolution.
Decision it feeds: Flags the image for further visual review by a human inspector.
Miss vs. false alarm: Miss is more costly. Missing actual spalling means potential damage is not flagged, while a false alarm primarily creates additional human review.
